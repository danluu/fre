# Rebar fast-plan frontier

This is a planning receipt derived from the frozen construction-admission report
and expanded manifest. It is not a benchmark result, planner rule, or permission
to inspect timing when selecting a plan.

The sum is descriptive only: Rebar times each job independently and may run it
many times. The large maxima make repeated suffix search and a general `O(QN)`
table poor performance endpoints even though the latter is a bounded semantic
floor.

## Portfolio order

1. **Exact-literal whole-operation reducer.** One non-overlapping Two-Way/SIMD
   traversal should serve the 29 exact-literal jobs. Empty bytes-mode patterns
   require the explicit `N + 1` byte-boundary rule. Count and span-sum are
   separate operation identities even when one loop can compute both.
2. **Ordered finite-language reducer.** The next 17 packed-set jobs need exact
   leftmost-first tie breaking and non-overlapping progress. The obvious
   `aho-corasick` 1.1.4 `LeftmostFirst::find_iter` candidate is falsified as a
   general aggregate plan: its `next` restarts search at the prior end, and
   `[a^(N/2)b, a]` on `a^N` scales quadratically. A viable candidate is a
   reversed one-transition-per-byte literal transducer plus bounded reverse
   count/span recurrences, with distinct initial/progressed modes for empty
   suppression. A direct iterator needs a separately proved deterministic
   commit restriction. Empty alternatives, duplicates, and both prefix orders
   require forced complete-sequence oracle tests.
3. **One-byte class/dot reducers.** Several current literal-set-DFA jobs are
   semantically byte membership. Lower them directly to SIMD membership/count
   loops, not an enumerated literal DFA. Width-one matches make non-overlap and
   span-sum closed form; Unicode scalar classes are a different plan.
4. **Fixed-width product kernels.** Fixed sequences of byte classes/literals can
   use SIMD candidate generation plus exact verification and advance by the
   proved width. Eligibility must include a finite decision horizon and exact
   priority, not merely positive minimum width.
5. **Suffix/prefix-confirmation reducers.** Required-literal and reverse-suffix
   shapes can scan candidates once and confirm bounded context. The certificate
   must charge rescans and prove that a late-priority fallback cannot recreate
   the `(?:a+b|a)` quadratic adversary.
6. **General native aggregate floor.** Remaining K0 shapes need a one-pass
   bounded executor: specialized Thompson state sets, bounded lazy DFA, or a
   portfolio choice with an aggregate `O(QN)` work certificate. JIT code should
   specialize state transitions and SIMD prefilters rather than dispatch over
   bytecode. A bounded portable aggregate result remains the correctness
   fallback, not the performance claim.

## Promotion rule

A plan may route from canonical HIR and operation/profile/target identity only;
Rebar job name, input bytes, expected result, or measured timing cannot be a
selector. Promotion requires forced differentials for the complete match
sequence, exact build/search/code/storage limits, allocation and fallback
audits, cold and warm measurements, the frozen non-Rebar gate, and retained
losses. Pointwise Rebar qualification comes only after the semantic adapter and
timed boundary are exact.

The source counts can be regenerated by joining
`comparison/admission-frontier.json` on job ID with
`expanded/manifest.json`; both inputs carry committed SHA-256 identities.
